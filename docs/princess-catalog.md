# Disney Princess watch list

One watch list follows the 43 numbered entries in `universe/princess_release.txt`, preserving the supplied franchise grouping rather than resorting it by date. Series expand into their regular seasons. The linked [Disney Princess Wiki list](https://disneyprincess.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Disney_Princess_Films) was consulted as reference; no additional titles from its broader categories were automatically added. Unnumbered “see also” suggestions in the source are not separate entries.

TMDB supplies posters, directors, runtimes, and episode metadata. Film years retain the source's years; full dates are not invented. Series dates use episode metadata. Animated and live-action titles with the same name have distinct TMDB identities, including Aladdin (1992/2019), Mulan (1998/2020), and Cinderella (1950/2015).

Elena's four short collections are mapped to five episodes each from the parent series' specials. Coronation Day is retained as a standalone special, as requested by the numbered source, and excluded from the Season 3 episode collection to avoid duplicate tracking. Undated placeholder episodes 29–31 in the TMDB Season 3 response are excluded. Its implausible one-minute finale runtime is left unknown.

The approved Cinderella castle cover was generated with the built-in image tool, with the text “Disney Princess”, and is stored at `assets/cards/princess.png`.
